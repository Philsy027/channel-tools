Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 September 2026

Channel Tools is a personal command-line application with a single user: its author. It is not offered to anyone else and collects no information from anyone else.

What it accesses

With the account holder's explicit consent, it accesses their own YouTube account through Google's APIs, using these scopes:

  • youtube — to upload videos and update their metadata, thumbnails and visibility.
  • youtube.force-ssl — to add and update caption tracks.
  • yt-analytics.readonly and yt-analytics-monetary.readonly — to read the account holder's own channel statistics.

It only ever reads or writes data belonging to the account that authorised it. It does not access any other person's data.

What it stores, and where

OAuth refresh tokens are written to files on the author's own computer, with owner-only file permissions. Downloaded statistics reports are written to the same computer. Nothing is stored on any server, and there is no database, hosted backend or analytics service behind this application.

What it shares

Nothing. No data obtained from Google is transmitted to any third party, sold, or used for advertising. It is used solely to publish the account holder's own videos and to inform their own editing decisions.

Retention and deletion

Tokens and reports remain on the author's computer until deleted. Deleting the token files removes the application's access. Access can also be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions, which immediately invalidates the stored tokens.

Google API Services User Data Policy

This application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
